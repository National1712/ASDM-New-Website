# Anti-Copy Register (ANTI-COPY-REGISTER.md)

This register lists distinctive visual layouts, copywriting taglines, brand illustrations, and interactive metaphors of competitors that **must not be copied or reproduced** in the ASDM website redesign. This ensures the final product remains original, compliant, and unique.

---

## 🔒 Distinctive Competitor Patterns to Avoid

### 1. IIDE (Indian Institute of Digital Education)

- **Brand Metaphors**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: The diagonal color swipe separator blocks and the specific dark-blue/neon-green accents interface.
  - _Do Not Copy_: The "University-style Admissions Timeline" visual tracker.
- **Copywriting Taglines**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: "India’s #1 Digital Marketing Institute" or "Become a Digital Marketing Specialist in 4 Months".
- **Visuals**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: The specific cartoon-style mascot vector illustrations displaying laptops.

### 2. Digital Sandip Academy

- **Brand Metaphors**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: The neon yellow buttons with black text on dark-gray backgrounds.
  - _Do Not Copy_: The continuous scrolling marquee of student salary cards.
- **Copywriting Taglines**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: "Learn from Google Partner Agency" or "Earn 50k freelancing" (also disallowed under our ASCI claims lock rules).

### 3. TOPS Technologies

- **Brand Metaphors**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: The orange header banner combined with blue CTA buttons.
  - _Do Not Copy_: The "Placement Ribbon" styled as a diagonal ribbon overlay on trainer images.
- **Visuals**:
  - _Do Not Copy_: Stock photography displaying western corporate office spaces.

### 4. Apple, Stripe, and Premium Reference UI Elements

- **Do Not Copy Stripe's Skewed Backgrounds**: Do not implement Stripe's exact diagonal skewed CSS divider angles (`transform: skewY(-12deg)`).
- **Do Not Copy Apple's Hardware Outlines**: Do not frame student portfolio websites inside Apple device mockups (MacBook or iPhone screen bezels).
- **Do Not Copy Linear's Glowing Borders**: Do not use Linear's specific glowing border hover effect or tech dark-mode cards.
- **Do Not Copy Vercel's Monochrome Grid**: Do not copy Vercel's exact triangle logo geometries or flat command-line preview containers.

---

## 🎨 General Safeguards for Originality

- **Copywriting Rule**: Write all headings and paragraphs based strictly on the factual content maps under `src/content/source/current-site/`.
- **Illustration Rule**: Use only verified local graphics from `public/assets/` or custom SVGs built to represent clean schematics. No stock vectors from competitor sites.
- **Visual Design Rule**: All color values, grid spacings, and layouts must be designed from the ground up inside the ASDM-specific design system (Phase 4), prioritizing a premium local feel.
