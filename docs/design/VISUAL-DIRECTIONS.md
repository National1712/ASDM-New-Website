# Visual Directions

Status: `PHASE 6 DIRECTION SELECTED`
Last updated: 2026-08-01

## A. Precision Editorial

- Strategic idea: Use strong typography, generous whitespace, and refined neutral surfaces to make ASDM feel premium and focused.
- Brand feeling: Confident, modern, clear, and professionally restrained.
- Colour philosophy: Neutral surfaces dominate; brand color appears only where attention or state needs it.
- Typography philosophy: One precise sans family with display strength and readable body performance.
- Layout behaviour: Clear containers, controlled line lengths, and grid alignment.
- Image treatment: Real local media only, cropped through stable aspect ratios.
- Motion behaviour: Minimal state transitions.
- Strength: Highest perceived quality and longevity.
- Risk: Can feel too quiet if future sections lack student energy.
- Suitability for ASDM: Strong base.

## B. Digital Energy

- Strategic idea: Add controlled color, layered surfaces, and dynamic geometry to signal current digital work.
- Brand feeling: Energetic, creative, youthful, and technologically aware.
- Colour philosophy: Blue leads; orange and teal are used as selective accents.
- Typography philosophy: Confident weights, compact labels, and strong numerals.
- Layout behaviour: Rhythmic clusters and layered proof areas without card clutter.
- Image treatment: Bright real media with consistent ratios and restrained radii.
- Motion behaviour: Hover and disclosure transitions only.
- Strength: Adds momentum and memorability.
- Risk: Overuse could become noisy.
- Suitability for ASDM: Best as accent layer.

## C. Progressive Institution

- Strategic idea: Present ASDM as organized, credible, and future-ready through precise systems and strong hierarchy.
- Brand feeling: Trustworthy, capable, and mature without traditional-school styling.
- Colour philosophy: Deep neutrals and high-contrast dark surfaces support credibility.
- Typography philosophy: Clear hierarchy for scanning, comparison, and proof.
- Layout behaviour: Repeatable grids, calm columns, and strong spacing discipline.
- Image treatment: Local institutional and student-work assets only after approval.
- Motion behaviour: Mostly static with state-based interactions.
- Strength: Builds confidence for serious education decisions.
- Risk: Can become too formal if used alone.
- Suitability for ASDM: Strong credibility layer.

## Recommended Hybrid: ASDM Signature System

Use Precision Editorial as the base, Digital Energy as the accent, and Progressive Institution as the credibility layer. This is the only production design direction for Phase 6.
