# ASDM Statistic Integrity & Claims Report (CLAIMS-SUMMARY.md)

This report details inconsistencies, mismatches, and truth-in-advertising guidelines regarding ASDM statistics, partner numbers, and placement metrics across various landing pages.

## 🔒 Strict Verification & Integrity Guidelines (from AGENTS.md)

Per engineering rules:

- **Never invent** ASDM statistics, accreditations, student placements, trainer profiles, or marketing claims.
- **Factual Honesty**: We must represent numbers that are backed by administrative and registrar records.
- **Verification Rule**: Direct advertising claims involving students trained, placements delivered, partner networks, salary packages, or years in business must default to `REQUIRES INTERNAL BUSINESS PROOF` and require owner review before the copywriting phase.
