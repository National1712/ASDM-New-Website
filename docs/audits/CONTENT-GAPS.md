# Content Gaps & Copywriting Audit (CONTENT-GAPS.md)

This report details content gaps, copywriting issues, grammatical errors, and layout limitations discovered during the ASDM website audit.

## ✍️ Copywriting & Grammatical Quality Issues

Several critical public-facing pages suffer from poor copywriting, sentence fragments, and spelling mistakes:

1. **About Us Page Meta Description**:
   - _Current_: `Learn Digital Marketing In Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat. We are Providing 100% Job Placement After Completion the Program Also Learn Lead Generation Freelancing In Ahmedabad, Vadodara, surat`
   - _Errors_: Fragmented sentences; "Completion the Program" (missing "of"); lowercase "surat" at the end.

2. **Placement Page Header H1**:
   - _Current_: `70% Top Digital Marketing agency's senior Digital Marketers belongs to ASDM.`
   - _Errors_: Incorrect subject-verb agreement ("senior Digital Marketers belongs" should be "belong"); plural vs. possessive errors ("agency's" vs "agencies'").

3. **About Us Founder Biography**:
   - _Current_: `ASDM was found by MR. LOVE TYAGI in 2014 after their 5 Years of knowledge...`
   - _Errors_: "was found" (should be "was founded"); inconsistent pronoun use ("their" instead of "his").

4. **Locations (Contact) Naroda Address**:
   - _Current_: `nana chiloda, Airport Road...`
   - _Errors_: Lowercase name "nana chiloda" (should be capitalized: "Nana Chiloda").

---

## 🧩 Structure & Content Gaps

### 1. Program Duration Ambiguity

- The homepage statistics block lists course duration as "5 / 12 Months".
- The E-Commerce page specifies "Duration: 3 Months".
- The Surat location page FAQ claims "duration of this course is Approx 4 Months".
- _Gap_: There is no clear, detailed comparison page showing why duration varies (e.g. fast-track vs professional placement track vs local campus structures).

### 2. Missing Physical Campus Information

- The website actively targets Mumbai via `/digital-marketing-course-in-mumbai`, claiming "best offline and online digital marketing course in Mumbai".
- _Gap_: The contact form and contact details offer zero physical addresses or phone numbers in Mumbai. All actions route back to Ahmedabad and Surat, creating user distrust.

### 3. Faculty Profiles Gaps

- Aside from founder Mr. Love Tyagi, there are no detailed biography blocks or profile cards for other instructors, despite claims of having "25+ full-time trainers".

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Re-write meta descriptions and headings using high-quality copy to eliminate spelling and grammar errors.
- Clearly differentiate the program structure and duration tiers in a visual course comparison grid.
- Explicitly label the Mumbai course as "100% Live Online" to prevent user confusion regarding physical campus addresses.
