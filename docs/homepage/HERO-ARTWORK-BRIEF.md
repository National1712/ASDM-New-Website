# Hero Artwork Brief

Status: `PHASE 7.1 ARTWORK REQUIRED`
Last updated: 2026-08-01

## Desktop Artwork

- Preferred source size: `1800 x 2100 px`
- Approximate ratio: `6:7`
- Format: transparent PNG/WebP or layered design source
- Keep a clear safe area around the subject for responsive cropping.
- Do not embed the homepage headline in the artwork.
- Do not include fake claims, ratings, placement percentages, packages, certification logos, or rankings.

## Mobile Artwork

- Preferred source size: `1080 x 1350 px`
- Approximate ratio: `4:5`
- Separate mobile crop preferred.
- Maintain subject clarity at narrow widths.

## Creative Direction

- One ASDM student or young professional.
- Real practical digital-work context.
- Laptop or tablet visible.
- Optional project interface fragments.
- Controlled red-purple geometry.
- Maximum two floating labels.
- Clear internal safe area.

## Avoid

- Busy card collage.
- Blue blob background.
- Excessive shadows.
- Stock-business pose.
- Fake statistics.
- Unrelated political or internship image.
- Certificate logos, recruiter logos, star ratings, testimonial cards, or counters.
